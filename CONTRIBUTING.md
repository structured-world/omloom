# Contributing

Discuss a concrete change in an issue before implementation. Keep changes
scoped, document observable behavior, and include relevant verification.
Never include credentials or private infrastructure details.

Before a first pull request can be merged, sign the
[Structured World Contributor License Agreement](https://sw.foundation/cla).
The agreement is with Dmitry Prudnikov as a natural person, the Maintainer
defined in its terms. One signature covers contributions to the projects
defined in the agreement. Sign in with GitHub, confirm your email address and
sign; the `CLA` status is then provided by the application.

You retain copyright in your contributions. The CLA grants a license, including
the sublicensing rights described in section 2; it does not transfer ownership
of your contributions. Section 9 sets out the permitted assignment of the
agreement, including to a legal entity controlled by the Maintainer. Structured
World's support for Omloom does not itself transfer copyright ownership.

If you contribute as part of your job, your employer may also need to sign the
corporate agreement. See section 4 and the signing instructions on the CLA page.
